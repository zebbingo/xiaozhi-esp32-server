# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Xiaozhi Console collects, uses and retains your data when you use the service.

## Data Collection
- Registration and login details such as username, mobile phone number and verification codes.
- Device and log information to maintain service stability.

## Usage of Data
- Provide authentication and account management services.
- Improve product features and user experience.

## Data Retention and Protection
- Data is kept only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes above unless otherwise required by law.
- Reasonable security measures are taken to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure or loss.

## Contact
For questions about this policy, please open an issue in the project repository.
